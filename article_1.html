TENERE TALES • FROM THE PACK

Saturday dirt, worn tyres & Mannum

This is how a proper Tenere Tales story could look inside the new Gravel Hound site.

RIDE REPORT

A familiar crew, a good route and a few talking points

The new Gravel Hound article layout gives the story more room to breathe: stronger typography, larger imagery, useful pull-quotes, route links and obvious places for related videos or gear recommendations.

Tenere Tales remains a visible part of the brand rather than disappearing. It becomes the place for the character-driven ride stories — the people, the bikes, the mishaps and all the details that make the rides worth reading about.

Tenere Tales becomes a section of Gravel Hound, not a casualty of the move.

Where this gets interesting

Once migrated, an article can link directly to the route page, the bike page, a relevant workshop article and the latest Gravel Hound video. That creates a proper web of content instead of a collection of isolated posts.

Monetisation without ruining it

A small “Gear used on this ride” panel can sit below the story, and only when genuinely relevant. The same article can also contain a sponsor acknowledgement without making the entire piece feel bought.
